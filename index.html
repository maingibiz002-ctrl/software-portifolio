About Me

I graduated trained as a teacher, where my core craft was breaking complex ideas down until they clicked for someone else. Even back then, I had an unrelenting curiosity for technology—not just using tools, but understanding how systems actually work beneath the surface.

After graduation, I channeled that enthusiasm directly into software development. Instead of relying on shortcuts, I committed to building from first principles—writing raw code, debugging systems line by line, and turning real-world problems into working digital solutions.

Today, my educator's mindset shapes everything I build. Whether I am engineering clean software, mentoring aspiring developers, or collaborating with founders, I focus on clarity, structure, and shipping work that genuinely helps people.
